About Page 324

Page 324 publishes probabilistic forecasts for English league football: match results, finishing positions, promotion, relegation and other season outcomes.

The name comes from Ceefax, the BBC’s pre-internet text information service. Football supporters could enter a three-digit page number on their television to jump directly to results, fixtures and tables. Page 324 was the page for the Premier League table.

The aim here is similar in spirit: to provide compact, useful information about where the league stands and where it might be going.
