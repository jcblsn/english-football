# Research principles

## Claim levels and negative evidence

Interpret evidence at the level of the claim under test. The information hypothesis, its representation, its estimator and the deployable forecast are different claims. A failed out-of-sample comparison rejects deployment of that implementation. It is strong evidence against the information hypothesis only when materially different credible representations, including high-information or oracle conditions where feasible, also show little attainable signal. A plausible mechanism does not justify indefinite iteration after repeated informative failures. State in advance which claim an experiment can update and which result will stop or redirect the work.

Prefer coherent, reusable mechanisms over special cases, even when a heuristic gives a small empirical gain. Empirical improvement is necessary for deployment. It is not sufficient to justify poor architecture.
